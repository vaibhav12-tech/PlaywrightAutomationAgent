## Story Summary

REVA consumer app must show a sticky Important Safety Information (ISI) bar fixed at the bottom of the viewport with condensed safety language, hide it when the full ISI in the footer is fully in view, re-show it when scrolling away, and scroll to the full ISI when the sticky bar is selected. Full ISI must appear in the footer on all in-scope pages. UI-only; no backend dependency.

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
