## Story Summary

Admin opens a single **Edit Staff Member** modal from the staff row overflow (**Edit Role & Permissions**). First/Last/Email are view-only; Practice Role, Permissions, and Locations are editable together. Practice Role has six values (independent of Permission). Permission Admin → Locations disabled “All locations are accessible to admin.” Permission Team Member → per-location Ship To multi-select (min 1). Update atomically updates Person Account + Affiliations; no separate Edit Location Access flow. Failed update is not partial.
