## Story Summary

After the provider clicks Continue on checkout (BL10-521), the **Pending** screen shows a loading animation and next-step instructions while a confirmation SMS is sent to the patient. The screen shows the destination phone number, Resend, copyable support email/phone, and Cancel. On patient approval the UI auto-advances to completion. Added error states: Patient Denied, Approval Request Expired, Could not reach patient, Generic error. UI-only; copy is placeholder.
